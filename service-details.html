Wardrobe & Style Guide

Coordinating clothing is essential to achieve a unified editorial aesthetic. Our standard recommendations include:

  • Color Palette: Stick to earthy neutrals, creamy ivory, tan, slate, soft sage, or pastel tones. Avoid heavy primary blues, reds, and neon pigments.
  • Textures & Layers: Mix fabrics (linen, heavy knitwear, lace, denim) to create visual dimension without busy checkered or floral patterns.
  • Logos & Prints: Avoid large graphic fonts, matching branding tags, or fine parallel stripes that cause camera distortion.
